State and Motion Prototype Plan

Purpose and state map

Specimen User need States Visual/ motion feedback
Action Help users activate the primary action and understand where it leads. Default, hover, focus-visible, and active. Color, shadow, and a decorative arrow provide visual feedback.
Selection Let users select an optional choice and recognize that it is selected. Unchecked, checked, and keyboard focus. Border emphasis, checkmark indicator, and background treatment.
Disclosure Let users reveal additional information when needed. Closed and open. Native disclosure indicator and a short opening animation.

Effect decisions

Effect 1 and purpose: A subtle gradient will make the action link stand out from surrounding content and help users recognize it as an interactive element.

Effect 2 and purpose: Shadows and border emphasis will help distinguish the selected checkbox option and separate the disclosure content from the surrounding page.

What remains with effects removed: All text, labels, instructions, checkbox functionality, and disclosure content will remain understandable and usable even when decorative effects are removed.

Motion decisions

Transition properties and trigger: **Transition properties and trigger:** The action link uses explicitly named transition properties for its background color, text color, shadow, and transform. Its arrow also transitions when hovered. The checkbox choice transitions its background color, border color, and shadow. These transitions provide feedback as users interact with the prototypes. No `transition: all` declaration is used.

Keyframe purpose and trigger: A short fade-and-rise animation will provide visual feedback when the disclosure is opened. The animation will run once and will not hide or delay access to the disclosure content.

Reduced-motion alternative: When prefers-reduced-motion is enabled, the disclosure animation will be removed and transitions will be minimized. All interactive states, focus indicators, and selected-state feedback will remain visible and functional.
